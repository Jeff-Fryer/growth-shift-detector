Growth Shift Detectorby Jeff Fryer

Growth Shift Detector is built around a research method, not just a prompt. It looks for changes in public evidence, checks whether apparently different sources trace back to the same event, looks for evidence against its own findings, and stays conservative when the evidence isn’t strong enough.

The research prompt is simply a portable way to run that method in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another AI with web research.

AI can help us look wider and notice things sooner. It isn’t a replacement for judgment and senior leadership.

The research method

Growth Shift Detector uses five stages to separate meaningful commercial change from ordinary market noise:

Observe → Corroborate → Connect → Challenge → Surface

Observe
Establish what was true before looking for evidence that something changed.
Corroborate
Trace coverage back to the underlying event and look for independent evidence. Ten articles based on one press release count once.
Connect
Look for separate signals that point in the same direction while considering other explanations for what we’re seeing.
Challenge
Search for evidence against the emerging finding, not just evidence that supports it.
Surface
Report only what survives that process and use the lowest verdict the evidence supports. If nothing meaningful survives, say so.

A few of the evidence rules

  • Independent evidence matters more than repeated coverage.
  • Public evidence of orders and deployment matters more than announcements.
  • Repeat commercial activity matters more than first activity when it is publicly evidenced.
  • Hiring can indicate intent, but it does not prove adoption.
  • Funding creates capacity to move, but it does not prove demand.
  • A partnership without evidence of deployment is not proof of commercialization.
  • Evidence against a finding matters, too.
